# Omora homepage — layout spec

Reference build: design/homepage-v3-mockup.html (live at omora-mockup.vercel.app).
Port this structure into the Next.js app. Image slots stay blank until the shoot.

## Nav
Wordmark (Cormorant, uppercase, 0.35em tracking) · What we do · Work · About · Start a project

## 1. Hero
- H1: Your entire online presence. Handled.
- Sub: Websites, AI call answering, booking systems, and the media and ads around them. Built for local businesses, by one team.
- CTAs: Start a project (primary), See what we do
- [SLOT] work reel, full width

## 2. Team video
Short intro beside a video slot.

## 3. What we do
Four equal cards, image slot on each, each links to its own page:
- Websites — the site itself, built to turn visitors into inquiries
- Media & content — photo, video, scripts, posts
- Traffic & ads — paid, local search, tracking
- Smart systems — call answering, follow-up, booking, lead research, internal tools
Plus "View all services" and a chip row of the specifics inside those buckets.

## 4. About
Short, two paragraphs. Team photo slot.

## 5. Recent work
Three projects. Each: screenshot slot, category, name, then
Problem / Built / Result on three labeled lines. Result carries the number.
Full project list lives on the Work page, not the homepage.

## 6. Contact
Form (name, email or phone, what's the problem), button "Start the project".
Text-us number beside it.

## Footer
Omora wordmark, Instagram, Terms, Privacy.

## Rules
- Never "ticket". It's "Start a project" everywhere.
- Every project needs a real screenshot and a real result number. No filler copy.
- Palette: bg #0D0908, raised #181110, line #2C211E, violet #C18CFF / #8B4FE6 / #6B32C4.
- Fonts: Cormorant (wordmark only), Libre Franklin (headings), Inter (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels). No weight 800.

## Not this pass
Full /work page, testimonials, pricing page, OG image.
